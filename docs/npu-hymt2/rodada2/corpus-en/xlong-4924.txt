On Tuesday morning, officers responded to a call regarding a traffic accident near the intersection of Main Street and Oak Avenue. The driver of a blue sedan failed to stop at a red light and collided with a delivery truck. No injuries were reported, and both vehicles sustained minor damage. The officer collected the driver's documents, interviewed two witnesses, and issued a citation for disregarding the traffic signal. According to the witnesses, the sedan was traveling at a moderate speed when it entered the intersection. The truck driver stated that he had the right of way and could not avoid the collision. Traffic cameras at the corner recorded the incident, and the footage was later reviewed by the department. The officer also checked the sedan driver's license status through the mobile terminal and confirmed that it was valid. Both drivers were cooperative throughout the process. The scene was cleared after about forty minutes, and the towing service removed the sedan once the paperwork was complete. A copy of the report was given to each party, and the case number was provided for insurance purposes. Later that same day, a second unit was dispatched to a residential address following a noise complaint. Upon arrival, the officers spoke with the resident, who explained that a family gathering had gone on longer than expected. The officers advised the resident about the city ordinance on nighttime noise and asked the guests to lower the volume. The resident apologized and promised to keep the music down. A short time later, the officers received a request for backup from a nearby patrol car that had stopped a vehicle with expired registration. The driver explained that he had recently purchased the car and was still waiting for the new plates to arrive by mail. The officers verified the bill of sale, confirmed the purchase date, and issued a warning instead of a citation. The vehicle was released at the scene. During the return to the station, dispatch reported another incident on the highway involving a stalled car blocking the right lane. The officers arrived, placed cones around the vehicle, and waited with the driver until a tow truck arrived. The driver was grateful for the assistance and mentioned that he had called the roadside service company twice but had not received a clear estimate of the arrival time.
